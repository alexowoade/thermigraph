## 8. Pilot Validation - Integrity Statement

This section is intentionally empty.

There is no pilot testimonial, no expert quote, and no named reviewer
here, because there is no real pilot, no real reviewer, and no real
crew to quote yet. No fabricated quote, reviewer, or pilot has been
added, and none will be added. The scripted demo names in `triage.py`
(Ahmed, Ramesh, Bilal) are fictional scenarios used to show the
name-redaction guard working; they are not real workers, and no real
crew is named or protected by this section because no real crew has
taken part.

When a pilot is conducted, it will be documented here with the
participant's name, role, site, and date, and a verbatim quote given
with that person's consent. Until then, this section stays empty
rather than filled with invented validation.

What the project *does* rest on is published evidence and passing
tests, documented in the brief at `clinical-evidence-brief.md` (repo
root): the Liljegren et al. 2008 WBGT model
(doi:10.1080/15459620802310770), NIOSH Criteria 2016-106, ISO 7243
(reviewed in Parsons 2006, *Industrial Health*, 44, 368-379), and the
Florida farmworker acute kidney injury study (*Journal of Health Care
for the Poor and Underserved*, 2025, 36(2), 590-604). That brief
states what the evidence proves and what it does not prove: the WBGT
value is a weather-derived estimate, not a calibrated meter reading;
triage is preventive occupational triage, not a medical diagnosis;
and the Cumulative Thermal Strain Index red line (12) is a prototype
score, not a published clinical cutoff.
