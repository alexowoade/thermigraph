Evidence & Limits

11. Clinical Evidence & Validation

ThermiGraph is a prototype. This section states what the science supports, what the code has been tested for, and what is not proven. No expert quote, clinician name, or real pilot is claimed here.

WBGT formula

Outdoors, in direct sun: WBGT = 0.7 × natural wet bulb + 0.2 × black globe + 0.1 × dry bulb. In shade or indoors: WBGT = 0.7 × natural wet bulb + 0.3 × black globe. ThermiGraph uses the outdoor weighting, with wet bulb by the Stull formula and black globe estimated from solar radiation. That is a prototype simplification of the Liljegren model below, not a calibrated meter reading.

NIOSH work limits

Recommended Exposure Limits, acclimatised workers, continuous work: Light 30 °C, Moderate 28 °C, Heavy 26 °C, Very heavy 25 °C. Unacclimatised workers are 2–3 °C lower. Above the limit, NIOSH prescribes work/rest scheduling: 45 work / 15 rest, then 30 / 30, then 15 / 45, then stop. ThermiGraph uses the acclimatised limits and the 45/15 and 30/30 steps.

Multi-day strain and kidney injury

The ThermiGraph claim is that heat stroke and acute kidney injury come from multi-day cumulative strain, not a single hot afternoon. In the Florida farmworker study below, over half of workers started the day dehydrated, 81% were dehydrated after a day of work, and one third had acute kidney injury at least once during three summer workdays. The odds of acute kidney injury rose 47% for each 5-degree increase in heat index, and kidney injury prevalence rose from 21% to 43% over three years in fernery workers. That supports tracking strain over 6 days and flagging failed overnight recovery when the night minimum stays above 28 °C.

Validation tests: 8 / 8 passed

Validation tests passed in the 2026-11-16 check, run with no network, no Gemini, and no patient data:

  • Stull wet bulb: 29.1 °C, 43% RH gives a wet bulb of 20.3 °C.
  • WBGT formula: 0.7 / 0.2 / 0.1 weighting on fixed wet bulb, globe and dry bulb inputs.
  • Live Mussafah WBGT estimate: 25.4 °C at 29.1 °C, 43% RH, 631 W/m² solar.
  • NIOSH limits: Light 30, Moderate 28, Heavy 26, Very heavy 25 °C.
  • Work/rest ratios: 60/0 up to 25 °C, 45/15 at 25–26 °C, 30/30 at 26–28 °C, no outdoor work above 28 °C.
  • Daily strain points: 1 point at 25.4 °C, 3 points above 28 °C, plus 1 point for recovery debt.
  • Recovery debt: night minimum above 28 °C flags yes, at or below 28 °C flags no.
  • Triage model: the sample note returns Critical, with vomiting and stopped sweating in the symptoms.

Limitations, in plain words

  • WBGT is estimated from weather data, not measured with a globe meter.
  • Globe temperature is estimated from solar radiation, not measured.
  • Clothing correction (ISO 7243 Annex C, +0 / +1 / +2 / +4 °C) IS applied via the Clothing / PPE selector in Section 2. Acclimatisation, age, and medical history are still not modelled.
  • The cumulative score of 12 is a prototype score, not a clinical threshold.
  • Triage is structured extraction from a typed note, not a medical diagnosis.
  • No real crew, no real shift, and no real meter reading are claimed.
